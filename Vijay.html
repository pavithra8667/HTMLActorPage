vijay

Vijay, born on June 22, 1974, is an Indian actor, politician, and playback singer, primarily known for his work in Tamil cinema. He is one of the highest-paid actors in India and has gained immense popularity with films like Thuppakki and Mersal. Recently, he announced his retirement from acting and entered politics with the founding of Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) in 2024, aiming to address issues like corruption and social inequality.
